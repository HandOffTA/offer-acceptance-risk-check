# Offer Acceptance Risk Check

> Part of the [HandOffTA](https://github.com/HandOffTA) portfolio: practical AI tools for hiring teams.

## What It Does

Answer a few questions about a candidate's situation and get an offer acceptance risk level, plus talking points to address it.

## The Problem It Solves

Late-stage declines are expensive. Recruiters often sense the risk (competing offers, counteroffers, relocation concerns) but don't have a consistent way to assess it or prepare for the close.

## Who It's For

Recruiters and hiring managers heading into the offer stage, especially for hard-to-fill or high-priority roles.

## How It Works

1. **Answer a short questionnaire.** Competing offers, current employer counteroffer risk, comp gap, relocation, motivation, and timeline.
2. **Assess the risk.** The answers are weighed to produce a Low, Medium, or High risk level.
3. **Explain the drivers.** You see which factors are driving the risk.
4. **Get talking points.** Claude drafts specific talking points and next steps to help close the candidate.

## Tools Used

- Claude API
- Zapier, or
- Lovable
